How the Follow-up Cycle Operates

A transparent 3-stage statutory continuum ensuring no trained candidate is left unaccounted for.

01 Day 0 to 30

Certification & Onboarding

Candidate completes training course under MSSDS or approved partner institute. MahaKaushalya automatically assigns an encrypted digital outcome card and registers the trainee's primary mobile and WhatsApp handle.

badge Generates Unique MahaKaushal Candidate ID
02 Month 3 • Month 6 • Month 12

Automated Multi-Channel Check-ins

Automated SMS, interactive WhatsApp bots, and District Skill Committee IVR outreach prompt the candidate to submit salary slips, offer updates, or current occupational status in Marathi or English with one-tap verification.

mark_chat_read WhatsApp / SMS / IVR Triangulation
03 Year 1 Completion

Wage Verification & Certification

District verification officer cross-checks company HR proof or self-employment proofs. Candidate receives a formal Maharashtra State Outcome Completion Certificate, validating 1-year wage stability and career security.

verified State Official Employment Badge
